Wildlife timing

When to See the Great Migration in Kenya

Plan around river crossings and predator peaks; the timing shifts annually, so the best window depends on rainfall, movement and reserve conditions. For many travelers, the magic is not only in seeing the herds but in pairing them with the right lodge and guiding style.

Our specialists tailor this to your preferred pace, whether you want the most dramatic river crossings or a calmer, more luxurious stay with excellent wildlife viewing.

Travel insight

Why Fly-In Safaris Change Everything

Flying between camps maximizes wildlife time and minimizes transit fatigue. Instead of spending long hours on the road, you arrive refreshed and ready for early game drives, evening sundowners and a smoother start to each day.

For families, honeymooners and photographers, that efficiency often becomes the difference between a stressful trip and a deeply immersive one.

Coastal finale

The Art of Ending at Diani Beach

Pairing the savannah's rhythm with coastal relaxation creates an ideal recovery day and private beach experience. It gives the journey a natural rhythm: exhilaration in the bush, softness on the Indian Ocean, and time to linger in the luxury of both.

For many guests, a Diani finish is the perfect counterpoint to a high-energy safari, offering calm, privacy and a beautiful final chapter.

Kenya-based fly-in safari specialists offering bespoke African journeys designed for effortless luxury and authentic wildlife encounters.
