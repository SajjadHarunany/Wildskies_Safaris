Masai Mara

Big cats, sweeping savannahs and the Great Migration.

Overview

Masai Mara National Reserve is Kenya's most celebrated wildlife area. Each year the Mara hosts dramatic concentrations of grazing herbivores and their predators — a living, breathing wildlife spectacle anchored by the seasonal Great Migration of wildebeest and zebra.

What You'll See

  • Lions, cheetahs and leopards in open country
  • Large herds of wildebeest and zebra during migration
  • Birdwatching including raptors and marsh species

Best Time to Visit

Year-round wildlife viewing is excellent; peak drama is June–October when large-scale migration river crossings occur. For quieter luxury windows, consider November–February.

How to Get There

Most guests arrive by scheduled or charter light aircraft from Nairobi — a scenic 45–60 minute flight to the Mara airstrips followed by a short game-drive transfer to camp.

Suggested Activities

  • Early-morning and late-afternoon game drives
  • Balloon safaris at dawn (seasonal)
  • Private guided walks in conservancy areas
