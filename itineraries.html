DETAILED DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY

Day 1: Arrival & First Afternoon Game Drive
  • Check in at Nairobi Wilson Airport (WIL) at least 1 hour prior to scheduled departure.
  • Board scheduled flight taking off for Mara Serena Airstrip over the Great Rift Valley.
  • Meet upon landing by your dedicated professional safari guide in a private 4x4 Land Cruiser.
  • Enjoy an introductory game drive en route to Mara Serena Safari Lodge perched high on the hill.
  • Check in, settle into your accommodation, and enjoy a delicious lunch overlooking the Mara Plains.
  • Embark on an afternoon private game drive exploring the rich wildlife of the Mara Triangle.
  • Return to the lodge in the evening for dinner and overnight stay.

Accommodation: Mara Serena Safari Lodge | Meal Plan: Full Board (Lunch, Dinner)

Day 2: Peak Wildlife & Great Migration Exploration
  • Early morning private game drive when predators and plains game are most active.
  • Return to the lodge for a hearty hot breakfast while admiring panoramic savannah views.
  • Mid-day at leisure: relax by the swimming pool, visit the spa, or enjoy lodge facilities.
  • Savor lunch at the lodge dining restaurant.
  • Head out on an afternoon private game drive through sunset, seeking lions, leopards, and wildebeest herds.
  • Return for dinner and overnight stay at Mara Serena Safari Lodge.

Accommodation: Mara Serena Safari Lodge | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 3: Full-Day Masai Mara Game Tracking
  • Enjoy breakfast at the lodge before setting off on an extensive full-day game drive experience.
  • Full day exploring key wildlife hotspots along the Mara River with your private 4x4 Land Cruiser.
  • Option for a bush picnic lunch out in the reserve or return to the lodge for lunch (subject to guest preference).
  • Prime opportunities to observe the Big Five, cheetahs, crocs, and massive seasonal migration movements.
  • Return to the lodge for relaxation, gourmet dinner, and overnight stay.

Accommodation: Mara Serena Safari Lodge | Meal Plan: Full Board (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner)

Day 4: Farewell Mara & Return Flight to Nairobi
  • Enjoy breakfast at the lodge and complete check-out formalities.
  • Transfer to Mara Serena Airstrip with a final short game viewing en route.
  • Board scheduled flight from Mara Serena back to Wilson Airport (WIL) in Nairobi.
  • Arrive at Wilson Airport, marking the conclusion of your luxury safari journey.

Accommodation: Check-out | Meal Plan: Breakfast
