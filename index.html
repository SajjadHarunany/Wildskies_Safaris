Featured Destinations

Kenya's Premier Safari Routes

Four extraordinary destinations, one seamless fly-in experience. Begin with the wild theatre of the Mara, explore the iconic plains of Amboseli, discover the raw wilderness of Tsavo, then finish on pristine Diani Beach.

Great Migration + Big Cats

Masai Mara - Where the Wild Calls

The Masai Mara is Kenya at its most cinematic: rolling grasslands, golden light and predators moving through one of Africa's richest wildlife theatres. Here, every game drive carries the possibility of lions on the hunt, cheetahs on the plains and evenings that glow long after sunset.

Why it belongs on your list: Home to the seasonal Great Migration and one of the world's highest densities of big cats, the Mara is a must for peak safari drama.

Explore Masai Mara

Kilimanjaro Views + Tuskers

Amboseli - Giants Beneath Kilimanjaro

Amboseli is a place of elemental beauty, where vast skies frame herds of elephants crossing the plains below Mount Kilimanjaro. Its open landscapes create unforgettable photographic moments, especially at sunrise when the mountain appears in quiet grandeur.

Why it belongs on your list: Amboseli is famed for Africa's largest tuskers and the iconic Kilimanjaro backdrop that defines the classic East African safari image.

Explore Amboseli

Remote Scale + Red Elephants

Tsavo - The Red Heart of the Wild

Tsavo is immense, raw and deeply atmospheric, a wilderness of lava flows, red earth, baobabs and endless horizons. It offers a more remote safari rhythm, perfect for travelers drawn to space, solitude and landscapes that feel ancient.

Why it belongs on your list: Tsavo's famous red elephants, dusted in the park's iron-rich soil, make it one of Kenya's most distinctive safari destinations.

Explore Tsavo

Bush-to-Beach Ease

Diani Beach - Barefoot Luxury on the Indian Ocean

Diani is Kenya's coastal exhale: white sands, warm turquoise water, swaying palms and days that drift between ocean swims and candlelit dinners. It is the perfect finale after the intensity of the bush, offering softness, privacy and effortless restoration.

Why it belongs on your list: Diani creates a seamless bush-to-beach escape, pairing big wildlife days with pristine Indian Ocean relaxation.

Explore Diani Beach
