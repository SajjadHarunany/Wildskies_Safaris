Safari Planning

Kenya Safari Questions, Answered

The practical details travellers ask us most often before booking a private fly-in safari in Kenya.

What is a fly-in safari in Kenya?

A fly-in safari replaces long road transfers with scheduled or private light aircraft. Instead of a six-hour drive from Nairobi to the Masai Mara, you take a scenic 45 to 60 minute flight to a bush airstrip and begin game viewing the same afternoon. You spend less time in transit, arrive at camp rested, and gain more active wildlife hours across the same number of days.

When is the best time to see the Great Migration in the Masai Mara?

Peak migration drama generally falls between June and October, with the most reliable river crossings from July to September. The precise timing shifts each year with rainfall and herd movement, so we plan around live reports rather than fixed dates. November to February offers quieter luxury windows with excellent resident predator viewing.

How do you reach the Masai Mara, Amboseli and Tsavo?

All three are served by light aircraft from Nairobi Wilson Airport. The Masai Mara is a 45 to 60 minute flight, Amboseli roughly 45 minutes, and Tsavo a short hop toward the coast. Amboseli and Tsavo can also be reached by road, though flying is far more efficient for short itineraries.

Are Wild Skies itineraries private and customisable?

Every itinerary is private and built around your party. Published packages are a starting point: we adjust duration, lodges, pace and routing around your interests, dietary needs, family dynamics, photography goals or a celebration. You are never sharing a vehicle or a guide with another booking.

Can you combine a safari with a beach holiday in Diani?

Yes, and it is our signature finish. Diani Beach on Kenya's south coast is reached by direct light aircraft, so you can move from a morning game drive to an afternoon on the Indian Ocean in a single day. Bush-to-beach itineraries typically finish with three to four nights on the coast.

How far in advance should I book a Kenya safari?

For peak migration season, six to nine months ahead is sensible because the best camps have limited rooms. For travel in the quieter January to March and November windows, three to four months is usually comfortable. If your dates are fixed around a milestone celebration, earlier is always better.
