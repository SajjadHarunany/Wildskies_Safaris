Featured Destinations

Masai Mara, Amboseli, Tsavo and Diani handpicked for exceptional wildlife and authentic coastal finishers.

Big Cats & Migration

Masai Mara

Rolling savannahs, incredible predator action and the world-famous Great Migration make the Mara the classic East African safari stage. Expect dramatic river crossings, early-morning game drives and optional hot-air balloon safaris.

Learn more about Masai Mara

Kilimanjaro Views

Amboseli

Amboseli is synonymous with large elephant herds and stunning views of Mount Kilimanjaro. The park's open plains provide exceptional photographic opportunities, especially early and late in the day.

Learn more about Amboseli

Vast & Remote

Tsavo

Tsavo's wide-open spaces, dramatic landscapes and iconic red elephants offer a more remote safari experience. Ideal for off-grid camps, night drives and a deeper sense of wilderness.

Learn more about Tsavo

Coastal Finale

Diani Beach

White-sand beaches, coral reefs and relaxed coastal lodges. Diani is the perfect way to end a safari, with private villas, snorkeling and serene days beside the Indian Ocean.

Learn more about Diani

Kenya-based fly-in safari specialists offering bespoke African journeys designed for effortless luxury and authentic wildlife encounters.
