Amboseli

Elephant herds framed by the silhouette of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Overview

Amboseli National Park is famed for its close-up elephant encounters and unrivalled views of Mount Kilimanjaro on clear days. The park's wetlands and dry plains attract diverse wildlife and are ideal for photography.

What You'll See

  • Large, habituated herds of elephants and iconic tuskers
  • Birdlife drawn to the marshes and seasonal water sources
  • Classic East African predators and plains game

Best Time to Visit

June–March generally has excellent visibility and wildlife activity. For clearer Kilimanjaro views, plan around the drier months.

How to Get There

Fly-in options from Nairobi are commonly used; driving from Nairobi is also possible (approx 4–5 hours) and offers scenic highland views en route.

Suggested Activities

  • Guided game drives timed for Kilimanjaro photography
  • Visit local Maasai communities (responsibly and by arrangement)
  • Birding walks around marshy areas
