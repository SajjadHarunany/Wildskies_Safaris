Tsavo

Immense landscapes, red earth and iconic tuskers.

Overview

Tsavo is one of Kenya's largest parks, split into Tsavo East and Tsavo West. Its raw, wide-open landscapes and striking geology along with the famous red elephants create a sense of scale and remoteness.

What You'll See

  • Red-dust elephants and large mixed herbivore herds
  • Lava flows, river gorges and baobab trees
  • Opportunities for night drives and walking safaris in select areas

Best Time to Visit

Wildlife viewing is good year-round; the drier months (June–October) concentrate game near water sources.

How to Get There

Fly-in charters from Nairobi are the most efficient way to access remote camps; driving is possible but distances are long and roads can be rough.

Suggested Activities

  • Full-day wilderness drives and photographic safaris
  • Guided walks and conservancy experiences
  • Visit the Mzima Springs (Tsavo West) and scenic viewpoints
